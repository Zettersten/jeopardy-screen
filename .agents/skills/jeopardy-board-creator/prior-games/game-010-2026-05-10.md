## Categories & Key Topics Covered

### CREATURE FEATURE
- kiwi bird — only bird with nostrils at tip of beak, New Zealand national symbol
- flamingos — collective noun "flamboyance"
- elephant — 22-month gestation, longest of any land mammal
- sperm whale — 90-minute breath hold, largest brain of any animal
- octopus — three hearts, blue blood, two-thirds of neurons in arms

### BLAST OFF
- NASA — Apollo 11, first humans on Moon, July 1969
- Proxima Centauri — closest star, 4.24 light-years
- Hubble Space Telescope — 1990 launch, Pillars of Creation, Deep Field
- Saturn — 274 confirmed moons as of 2025, most in solar system
- James Webb Space Telescope — December 2021, 1.5 million km from Earth, infrared

### DRIVE-THRU
- McDonald's — golden arches, 69 million customers daily, 100+ countries
- Wendy's — Dave Thomas, named after daughter, 1969, Columbus Ohio, square patties
- Taco Bell — Glen Bell, 1962, Downey California, 19-cent tacos
- Subway — Fred DeLuca, Peter Buck, 1965, 17 years old, $1,000 loan
- KFC — Harland "Colonel" Sanders, franchised at 62, Corbin Kentucky

### BODY TALK
- bones — 206 in adults, 270–300 in babies
- pancreas — produces insulin and digestive enzymes, behind stomach
- Eustachian tubes — Bartolomeo Eustachi, connect middle ear to throat
- masseter — jaw muscle, strongest pound-for-pound, up to 200 lbs force
- liver — only major organ that regenerates; loses up to 75% mass; Prometheus myth

### QUOTE IT
- Hamlet — "To be, or not to be" Shakespeare soliloquy
- John F. Kennedy — "Ask not what your country…" 1961 inaugural address
- Franklin D. Roosevelt — "The only thing we have to fear is fear itself" March 4, 1933
- Lincoln Memorial — "I have a dream" delivered on its steps, August 1963 March on Washington
- Laurel Thatcher Ulrich — "Well-behaved women seldom make history" 1976 scholarly article

### AROUND THE WORLD
- Australia — Great Barrier Reef, northeastern coast, largest coral reef system
- Russia — 6.6 million square miles, world's largest country by land area
- Tuvalu — Pacific island nation, ~11,000 population, 2023 treaty with Australia, sea level threat
- Vatican City — 0.44 sq km, entirely within Rome, world's smallest sovereign state
- Suez Canal — completed 1869, ~120 miles, Egypt, Mediterranean to Red Sea
