## Categories & Key Topics Covered

### OLD TESTAMENT
- Noah — ark dimensions 300×50×30 cubits, flood, two of every creature
- Joseph — Pharaoh's seven fat/thin cattle dreams, seven years famine and abundance, Egypt
- Deborah — only female judge, prophetess, palm tree Ramah/Bethel, Barak, Sisera
- Nehemiah — cupbearer to Artaxerxes, Jerusalem walls rebuilt in 52 days, Sanballat/Tobiah
- Obadiah — 21 verses, shortest OT book, judgment against Edom

### NEW TESTAMENT
- Lord's Prayer — Matthew 6:9-13, Luke 11:2-4, Our Father, daily bread, forgiveness
- Zacchaeus — chief tax collector Jericho, sycamore tree, Luke 19, half possessions to poor, fourfold
- Paul (Saul of Tarsus) — Damascus road, blinding light, Ananias healed him, 13 NT letters
- Seven churches — Revelation 2–3, Asia Minor, Laodicea lukewarm spit out
- Theophilus — Luke and Acts dedicated to him, name means "friend of God" in Greek

### ROCK & PUNK LEGENDS
- The Ramones — stage surname from Paul McCartney's alias "Paul Ramon," 1976 debut, $6,400 in 7 days
- Bohemian Rhapsody (Queen) — nearly 6 minutes, Kenny Everett radio plays forced release, best-selling UK single
- Fortunate Son (CCR/John Fogerty) — 1969, Vietnam draft-avoidance protest, commonly misused as patriotic anthem
- John Fogerty — 1988 self-plagiarism lawsuit (Old Man Down the Road / Run Through the Jungle), won by playing guitar for jury
- Brian May — Red Special guitar built with father Harold: Victorian fireplace mantel neck, oak table body, bicycle saddlebag tremolo arm, knitting needle tip

### BUILD IT RIGHT
- Two-by-four (2×4) — actual dimensions 1.5″ × 3.5″, standard wall stud
- OSB (oriented strand board) — replaced plywood as residential sheathing since 1980s, cross-oriented wood strands
- R-value — thermal resistance, higher = better insulation, IRC minimum by climate zone
- Balloon framing — 1830s–mid-20th century, continuous studs sill to roof plate (vs. platform framing)
- International Residential Code (IRC) — first published 2000, International Code Council, one- and two-family dwellings

### RAISING KIDS
- Tiger Mom (Amy Chua) — 2011 memoir, strict parenting, high-achieving daughters, global debate
- Diana Baumrind — UC Berkeley, 1960s, three styles: authoritarian/permissive/authoritative; authoritative = best outcomes
- "Back to Sleep" campaign — NIH 1994, back-sleeping infants, SIDS reduced >50% in following decade
- Harry Harlow — University of Wisconsin, late 1950s, wire vs. cloth surrogate mothers, rhesus monkeys, contact comfort over feeding
- Strange Situation — Mary Ainsworth 1969, toddler separation/reunion, secure/anxious-avoidant/anxious-ambivalent attachment

### SWEAT SCIENCE
- The Murph — CrossFit Hero WOD, Navy SEAL Lt. Michael Murphy killed Afghanistan 2005, 1-mile/100 pull-ups/200 push-ups/300 squats/1-mile with 20-lb vest
- Tabata Protocol — Izumi Tabata 1996, 8 rounds × 20s at 170% VO2max / 10s rest, first to improve both aerobic and anaerobic (28%)
- The snatch — Olympic weightlifting, one of two events (with clean and jerk), floor to overhead in one continuous movement
- Lactate threshold — ~80–85% max HR for trained athletes, blood lactate accumulates faster than cleared, sustainable vs. high-intensity boundary
- EPOC (excess post-exercise oxygen consumption) — elevated oxygen consumption post-workout, scientific basis of "afterburn effect"
