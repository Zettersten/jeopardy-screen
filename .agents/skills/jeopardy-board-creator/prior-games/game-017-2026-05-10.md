# Prior Game: From Pemberley to Flavortown

**ID:** game-017-2026-05-10
**Date:** 2026-05-10
**Occasion:** Game Night
**Audience:** Millennial adults who grew up on PBS, junk food, and the Food Network
**Grid:** 6 categories × 5 questions | Points: 100, 200, 300, 400, 500

## Categories & Key Topics Covered

### AFTER SCHOOL SPECIALS (90s PBS Kids)
- Barney the Dinosaur — purple, sang "I Love You," inexplicably terrifying to adults, PBS Kids
- Arthur — aardvark, Elwood City, sister D.W. (most chaotic character in children's TV)
- Bill Nye — bow tie, science enthusiasm, PBS, thermodynamics, The Science Guy
- Wishbone — Jack Russell terrier, imagined himself as Romeo/Don Quixote/Sherlock, made kids read classics
- Ghostwriter — 1992 PBS, Brooklyn teenagers, friendly ghost communicates by rearranging letters

### ALL I WANT FOR CHRISTMAS (Christmas movies)
- Home Alone — 1990, 8-year-old Kevin McCallister, booby traps, Wet Bandits, Macaulay Culkin
- A Christmas Story — 1983, Red Ryder BB gun, "you'll shoot your eye out," leg lamp
- Elf — 2003, Will Ferrell, human raised by North Pole elves, syrup and cotton balls
- It's a Wonderful Life — 1946, Frank Capra, George Bailey, angel Clarence earns wings
- National Lampoon's Christmas Vacation — Clark Griswold, 25,000 lights, Cousin Eddie, RV

### IT IS A TRUTH UNIVERSALLY ACKNOWLEDGED (Jane Austen)
- Mr. Darcy — Pride and Prejudice, Pemberley, insulting first proposal, Elizabeth Bennet rejects him
- Sense and Sensibility — Elinor (sensible) vs. Marianne (emotional/dramatic), Dashwood sisters
- Emma — Highbury matchmaker, Emma Woodhouse, catastrophically meddles in everyone's love life
- Northanger Abbey — naive Catherine Morland, Gothic horror novels, creepy estate, incorrect murder theory
- Persuasion — posthumously published, Anne Elliot, Navy captain Wentworth, famous love letter, final novel

### LAND OF THE FREE (American History)
- Boston Tea Party — 1773, 342 chests British tea, Boston Harbor, taxation protest colonists
- Louisiana Purchase — 1803, Thomas Jefferson, Napoleon, 828,000 sq miles, four cents per acre
- Manifest Destiny — 19th century doctrine, divinely destined westward expansion, settlers
- Emancipation Proclamation — January 1, 1863, Lincoln, Confederate states "forever free"
- Watergate — 1972 burglary, Democratic National Committee headquarters, Nixon resignation August 1974

### TRASH SNACK OLYMPICS (Junk Food)
- Oreo — best-selling cookie since 1912, cream-filled, twist/lick/dunk debate
- Doritos — fluorescent-orange triangular chips, powder residue, destroys keyboards and shirts
- Pringles — saddle-shaped stackable crisps, British court ruled not legally potato chips in UK
- Pop-Tarts — rectangular toaster pastries since 1964, breakfast food claim, many flavors
- candy corn — waxy triangular Halloween candy, least favorite, 35 million pounds sold annually anyway

### FLAVORTOWN, USA (Food Network Stars)
- Guy Fieri — Diners Drive-Ins and Dives, Flavortown, frosted tips, board shorts, never a bad meal
- Ina Garten — Barefoot Contessa, cooking for Jeffrey, store-bought is fine, really good olive oil
- Alton Brown — Good Eats, bow tie, food science, chemistry lecture disguised as cooking TV
- Bobby Flay — Iron Chef, many NYC restaurants, Throwdown!, perpetually competitive
- Emeril Lagasse — BAM!, kick it up a notch, Food Network original superstar, Essence spice blend, New Orleans
