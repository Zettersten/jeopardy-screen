## Categories & Key Topics Covered

### PSALMS & WISDOM
- Psalms — 150 chapters, longest book of the Bible, Israel's hymnal, Psalm 23
- Job — blameless man, Satan's wager with God, stripped of everything, restored twofold
- Proverbs — "Trust in the Lord with all your heart," Proverbs 3:5, credited to Solomon
- Ecclesiastes — "Vanity of vanities! All is vanity," Qohelet/Teacher, earthly pursuits
- Micah — 7 chapters, minor prophet, Micah 6:8 "act justly, love mercy, walk humbly"

### ACTS & THE EPISTLES
- Pentecost — Acts 2, Holy Spirit as tongues of fire, 50 days after Passover, disciples spoke new languages
- Philippians — Paul wrote from prison, Philippians 4:13 "I can do all things," rejoice always
- Romans — Paul's most systematic letter, Romans 8:1 "no condemnation for those in Christ Jesus"
- Philippi — Acts 16, Paul and Silas jailed, earthquake opens doors, jailer converts
- Hebrews — anonymous letter, Christ superior to Moses/angels/Levitical priesthood, Chapter 11 hall of faith

### GREAT NOVELS
- A Tale of Two Cities — Dickens, French Revolution, Sydney Carton, "best of times worst of times"
- 1984 — George Orwell, Oceania, Big Brother, doublethink, thoughtcrime, "War is peace"
- The Great Gatsby — Fitzgerald, Jazz Age, Jay Gatsby, Daisy Buchanan, Long Island Sound
- The Grapes of Wrath — Steinbeck, 1940 Pulitzer Prize, Joad family, Dust Bowl, Route 66
- The Metamorphosis — Kafka, 1915 novella, Gregor Samsa, transformed into giant insect

### OPENING LINES
- Moby-Dick — Melville, 1851, "Call me Ishmael," Captain Ahab, white whale
- Pride and Prejudice — Austen, "truth universally acknowledged," single man of fortune
- Rebecca — Daphne du Maurier, 1938, "Last night I dreamt I went to Manderley again," gothic, first wife
- The Hobbit — Tolkien, 1937, Bilbo Baggins, underground dwelling
- David Copperfield — Dickens, "Whether I shall turn out to be the hero of my own life," orphan's rise

### CAREER DAY
- résumé — 1-2 pages, work history, education, skills, reverse chronological order
- cover letter — explains interest and fit, accompanies job application
- Civil Rights Act (Title VII) — 1964, prohibits discrimination by race, color, religion, sex, national origin
- STAR method — behavioral interview technique, Situation Task Action Result, "Tell me about a time"
- Peter Principle — 1969, Laurence J. Peter, promoted to level of incompetence, stuck in hierarchy

### PLAYS & POEMS
- Macbeth — Shakespeare tragedy, Scottish general, murders King Duncan, Macduff's revenge
- "The Raven" — Edgar Allan Poe, tormented narrator, bird that says one haunting word
- A Midsummer Night's Dream — Shakespeare comedy, fairy forest, Puck, "Lord what fools these mortals be"
- "Song of Myself" — Walt Whitman, 1855, centerpiece of Leaves of Grass, 52 sections, democracy, body
- Death of a Salesman — Arthur Miller, 1949, Pulitzer + Tony Award, Willy Loman, American Dream collapse
