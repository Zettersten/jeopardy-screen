## Categories & Key Topics Covered

### MUPPETS
- Kermit the Frog — "It's Not Easy Being Green," host of The Muppet Show, Jim Henson's creation
- Michael Caine — Ebenezer Scrooge in The Muppet Christmas Carol (1992)
- Tim Curry — Long John Silver in Muppet Treasure Island (1996); also Rocky Horror, IT
- Animal — wild drum-obsessed Muppet, Dr. Teeth and the Electric Mayhem percussionist
- Gonzo — blue hook-nosed Muppet, revealed as alien in Muppets from Space (1999)

### 90S DISNEY MOVIES
- Aladdin — 1992, Agrabah, street thief, princess, Robin Williams as Genie, three wishes
- Beauty and the Beast — 1991, first animated Best Picture nominee, Belle, cursed prince, enchanted rose
- Mulan — 1998, Chinese woman disguises as male soldier, Imperial Army, ailing father
- Tarzan — 1999, Phil Collins wrote/performed entire soundtrack, boy raised by gorillas
- The Hunchback of Notre Dame — 1996, Victor Hugo, Quasimodo, Esmeralda, Judge Frollo, Paris

### LORD OF THE RINGS
- Sauron — Dark Lord, forged One Ring in Mordor, power bound to ring's survival
- Saruman — head of Istari order, Gandalf's superior, betrayed Fellowship, allied with darkness
- eleven (11 Oscars) — Return of the King swept 2004 Academy Awards in every nominated category, tied all-time record with Ben-Hur and Titanic
- Mount Doom — volcanic mountain in Mordor where One Ring was forged and must be destroyed
- Oxford — Tolkien was professor of Anglo-Saxon then Merton Professor at University of Oxford

### NARNIA
- wardrobe — Lucy Pevensie discovers Narnia through wardrobe in Professor's country house
- Aslan — great lion creator and king of Narnia, Stone Table sacrifice, resurrection, Christ allegory
- Christmas — White Witch's spell creates perpetual winter but never Christmas for 100 years
- seven — The Chronicles of Narnia comprises seven books (Magician's Nephew through Last Battle)
- Reepicheep — valiant talking mouse, sails to edge of world in Voyage of the Dawn Treader, enters Aslan's country

### WOODSTOCK
- Max Yasgur — dairy farmer in Bethel, New York, whose land hosted Woodstock 1969
- Jimi Hendrix — closed Woodstock with legendary psychedelic Star-Spangled Banner performance
- Janis Joplin — "Queen of Rock and Roll," powerful Woodstock set, died of drug overdose 1970
- The Who — Pete Townshend hit Abbie Hoffman with guitar when Hoffman stormed stage at Woodstock
- split screen — Woodstock 1970 documentary (dir. Michael Wadleigh) pioneered split-screen technique, won Oscar for Best Documentary

### NAME THAT DRUG
- acetaminophen — active ingredient in Tylenol, most-used medication in US, pain/fever relief
- ibuprofen — NSAID sold as Advil/Motrin, world's best-selling anti-inflammatory
- diphenhydramine — active ingredient in Benadryl; antihistamine causing drowsiness; also in sleep aids
- acetylsalicylic acid — aspirin; Felix Hoffmann at Bayer synthesized in 1897 from willow bark
- SSRIs (selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors) — class that Prozac/fluoxetine belongs to; block serotonin reabsorption
